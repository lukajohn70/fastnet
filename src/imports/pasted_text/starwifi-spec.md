1. Overall Style and Design LanguageTheme: Modern, professional, fast-loading, and high-contrast "Tech-Optimized" style. Prioritize function over excessive aesthetics.Target Audience: Users in Nigeria. Interface must be clear, intuitive, and simple. Mobile-first design for all customer screens.Color Palette:Primary: A deep, trustworthy Blue (#2563EB) for actions and branding.Secondary/Success: A vibrant Green (#10B981) for payment success and "Connected" states.Accent/Warning: A friendly Orange (#F97316) for notifications and plan options.Background: Clean White (#FFFFFF) with a very light Gray (#F3F4F6) for content separation.Text: Dark Gray (#1F2937) for headers and medium Gray (#6B7280) for body.Typography: Use standard, highly readable system fonts (like Inter, Montserrat, or even Nigerian-favored Open Sans). Optimize font sizes for mobile (Header: 18px+, Body: 14px-16px, Min. 12px for micro-copy).UI Elements: All components (buttons, input fields, tables) should have rounded corners (8px-12px border-radius). Use large, touch-friendly buttons.Spacing & Grid: Clean grid with generous padding and line height to minimize visual clutter. Mobile designs must have a distinct header and footer structure. Use a 4px or 8px grid system.2.
